Causal DiT

A DiT is a standard transformer whose tokens are noisy latent patches and whose output is a denoising target; making it causal — generating in chunks that attend only to finished chunks via a KV cache — is what turns a video generator into something that can run a control loop.

Intuition

Three renames turn a language model into a video diffusion model. Words become latent patches (compressed image pieces); next-token prediction becomes denoising (predict the flow-matching velocity for every noisy token — see Flow Matching); and the decoder-only causal mask becomes block-causal over chunks: a chunk of frames being denoised may read every finished chunk, never the future. Everything else — attention, MLPs, residuals, KV caching — is the same machinery LLMs use.

Latents first: why nothing here touches pixels

A 480P frame is ~400k pixels, mostly texture that carries no scene information. Video models therefore run every frame through a VAE and do all generation on the compressed latents; pixels appear only at the ends. The compression is spatial and temporal — in DreamZero’s Wan2.1 backbone, 8 latent frames stand in for 33 raw frames (6.6 s at 5 FPS). When a WAM paper says “video”, read “a small grid of latent vectors”.

The DiT block

Chop the latent grid into patches, project each to a fixed-width vector (a token), and stack the standard loop: attention (every token reads a weighted mix of the others), MLP, residuals. Two things make it a diffusion transformer: tokens are noisy latents, and the timestep ttt enters every block by scaling and shifting the normalization layers (the adaLN recipe of Peebles & Xie 2023) — one network thereby serves every noise level:

Attention over one shared sequence is also what makes multimodal joint modeling real rather than a slogan: video tokens, action tokens, text tokens and state tokens can sit in the same sequence, so (in a WAM) action tokens read the imagined future directly at every block. Nothing in the trunk is modality- or robot-specific — which is precisely why a video-pretrained trunk transfers.

Making it causal: chunks, KV cache, teacher forcing

A stock video DiT is bidirectional: it emits one fixed-length clip, every frame attending to every other. Two properties disqualify that for control: you need actions now, not after the clip; and fixed windows force frame subsampling that breaks alignment between video time and action time. The causal variant generates chunk by chunk — in DreamZero, a chunk is K=2K=2K=2 latent frames paired with H=48H=48H=48 actions, 1.6 s of world — under a block-causal mask: the chunk being denoised attends to all finished chunks and to itself, never the future. Finished chunks persist as a KV cache, so context grows almost for free.

Definition — Block-causal attention mask

Group the sequence into chunks 1,…,M1, \dots, M1,…,M. A query token in chunk iii may attend to a key token in chunk jjj iff j<ij < ij<i (finished, clean context) or j=ij = ij=i (its own window, bidirectional). Conditioning tokens (text, proprioception) are attendable from every chunk.

Training uses teacher forcing: previous chunks in context are clean ground truth. At inference a pure video generator must feed on its own imperfect outputs, and the mismatch compounds — autoregressive drift. A closed-loop robot model has an exit no offline generator has: after each chunk executes, the real camera frames can overwrite the predicted frames in the KV cache, restoring teacher-forcing conditions every chunk (DreamZero, §3.1 — toggle it in Fig. DIT-2’s inference tab). Cousin fixes in the offline world — noise injection on context, diffusion forcing — only soften the mismatch; observation overwrite deletes it, at the price of needing an environment that answers back.

Why does chunk-causal generation matter for a robot, beyond latency?
Alignment and statefulness. Bidirectional models need fixed-length windows, which forces video subsampling that distorts the correspondence between frame time and action time; causal chunks preserve native rates. And the KV cache is a visual memory: each new chunk conditions on up to 6.6 s of actual history — at inference, real observed history, thanks to the overwrite.
Where exactly does the LLM analogy break?
Two places. Tokens within a chunk are denoised jointly with bidirectional attention among themselves (an LLM is causal token-by-token), and the model predicts continuous velocities for latent patches rather than a softmax over a vocabulary. The causal structure lives at chunk granularity, not token granularity.
