Large Language Models

Large language models: the Transformer lineage through pretraining, scaling laws, alignment (RLHF and successors), open-weight releases, efficiency, and reasoning models.

0 read · 0 of 38 summarized

emergence2017–2019

  1. 2017 Attention Is All You Need architecture record only Introduced the Transformer, replacing recurrence with self-attention; the foundational architecture of virtually all LLMs.
  2. 2018 BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding pretraining record only Masked language modeling for bidirectional context; the ubiquitous pretrained encoder baseline across NLU tasks.
  3. 2018 Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training pretraining record only GPT-1 established the pretrain-then-finetune paradigm; one Transformer pretrained on unlabeled text beat task-specific models.
  4. 2019 Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer pretraining record only T5 reframed all NLP as text-to-text; one span-corruption-pretrained encoder-decoder fine-tunes to SOTA across diverse tasks.
  5. 2019 Fine-Tuning Language Models from Human Preferences alignment record only First applied RLHF to LMs - fine-tuned GPT-2 with learned rewards so outputs match human preferences.
  6. 2019 Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners scaling record only GPT-2 (1.5B) showed scaling model and data yields zero-shot abilities - coherent text, QA, translation with no task training.

foundational2020–2022

  1. 2020 Language Models are Few-Shot Learners scaling record only GPT-3 (175B) showed emergent few-shot in-context learning, popularizing prompting over fine-tuning as scale unlocks abilities.
  2. 2020 Learning to summarize from human feedback alignment record only Scaled RLHF to summarization; KL-regularized RL beat SOTA and human-written references, surpassing supervised learning.
  3. 2020 Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models scaling record only Quantified power-law loss scaling in model size, data, and compute, guiding how to allocate compute for LLM training.
  4. 2021 Finetuned Language Models Are Zero-Shot Learners alignment record only FLAN introduced instruction tuning - multi-task instruction fine-tuning gives strong zero-shot generalization.
  5. 2021 Improving language models by retrieving from trillions of tokens efficiency record only RETRO coupled a 7.5B LM with a 2T-token retrieval database, matching GPT-3-level performance with 25x fewer parameters.
  6. 2021 Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity architecture record only Mixture-of-Experts sparsity; first model past 1 trillion params without proportional compute, cementing MoE for scaling.
  7. 2022 BLOOM: A 176B-Parameter Open-Access Multilingual Language Model open models record only BigScience 1000+ researcher effort; first massive fully open multilingual LLM - a watershed for open science.
  8. 2022 Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models reasoning record only Few-shot step-by-step exemplars unlock multi-step reasoning in ~100B+ models - emergent ability tapped by prompting alone.
  9. 2022 Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback alignment record only Aligned Claude via a written principle set and AI self-critique (RLAIF), gaining harmlessness without human harm labels.
  10. 2022 FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness efficiency record only IO-aware exact attention kernel; made long-context training practical and became standard infrastructure in every LLM stack.
  11. 2022 LaMDA: Language Models for Dialog Applications alignment record only 137B model tuned for open-domain dialogue with safety/groundedness training; the shift to conversation-optimized LLMs.
  12. 2022 OPT: Open Pre-trained Transformer Language Models open models record only Meta openly released a GPT-3-class 175B model with weights and logs - a milestone in LLM democratization.
  13. 2022 PaLM: Scaling Language Modeling with Pathways scaling record only 540B dense Transformer trained across TPU pods; emergent few-shot reasoning beat prior SOTA on 28 of 29 benchmarks.
  14. 2022 Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models scaling record only Chinchilla scaling law - params and tokens scale together; 70B on 4x data beat 280B Gopher, recalibrating LLM training.
  15. 2022 Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback alignment record only InstructGPT: SFT + PPO RLHF; a 1.3B aligned model beat 175B GPT-3 on human preference - the pipeline behind ChatGPT.

expansion2023–2024

  1. 2023 Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model alignment record only RL-free alignment - optimizes the LM directly on preference data as its own reward model, matching RLHF with simpler training.
  2. 2023 Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models multimodal record only Natively multimodal family trained on text+images from scratch; Gemini Ultra surpassed GPT-4 on several benchmarks.
  3. 2023 GPT-4 Technical Report multimodal record only Large-scale multimodal model with human-level results on professional exams; new SOTA in reasoning and steerability.
  4. 2023 Llama 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models open models record only Open release up to 70B with RLHF-tuned chat models and a commercial license, setting the standard for open chat LLMs.
  5. 2023 LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models open models record only Open 7B-65B models; Chinchilla-style training on public data let 13B beat 175B GPT-3, igniting the open-source LLM wave.
  6. 2023 Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces architecture record only Selective state-space model matching Transformer quality with linear-time inference; the leading attention alternative.
  7. 2023 Mistral 7B efficiency record only 7.3B model beating Llama 2 13B on all benchmarks via grouped-query and sliding-window attention; design beats sheer scale.
  8. 2023 OpenAssistant Conversations -- Democratizing Large Language Model Alignment alignment record only Crowd-sourced 600k+ message dialogue corpus from 13k+ volunteers plus open chat models; community-driven RLHF proved viable.
  9. 2023 OpenChat: Advancing Open-source Language Models with Mixed-Quality Data alignment record only C-RLFT treats mixed-quality data sources as coarse rewards; its 13B beat all other 13B open models without preference labels.
  10. 2023 The Falcon Series of Open Language Models open models record only Apache-2.0 Falcon-40B topped the open leaderboard; Falcon-180B (3.5T tokens) was the largest open LLM of its moment.
  11. 2024 DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report efficiency record only 671B MoE (37B active) trained for ~$5.5M with FP8 + MLA; open frontier-class quality at a fraction of expected compute.
  12. 2024 Mixtral of Experts efficiency record only Open sparse MoE (8x7B, ~13B active) matching Llama 2 70B and GPT-3.5 while far faster; mainstreamed MoE for open models.
  13. 2024 OpenAI o1 System Card reasoning record only First production reasoning model trained with RL to think in long chains before answering; opened the test-time-compute era.
  14. 2024 The Llama 3 Herd of Models open models record only 405B-scale open frontier models with a full training/infra report; open weights reached GPT-4-class capability.

recent2025

  1. 2025 DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning reasoning record only Pure-RL (GRPO) elicited emergent long-form reasoning ("aha moment"); widely called the biggest training advance since InstructGPT.
  2. 2025 Kimi K2: Open Agentic Intelligence open models record only 1T-param open MoE post-trained for agentic tool use with large-scale synthetic trajectories; open agentic frontier marker.
  3. 2025 Qwen3 Technical Report open models record only Unified thinking/non-thinking open MoE family with a thinking budget; the dominant open-weight line of 2025.

Vision-Language Models

Vision-language models: visual backbones, contrastive image-text pretraining, frozen-LM bridging, visual instruction tuning, and open frontier VLM families.

0 read · 0 of 17 summarized

emergence2019–2021

  1. 2019 ViLBERT: Pretraining Task-Agnostic Visiolinguistic Representations for Vision-and-Language Tasks fusion record only Two-stream BERT-era vision-language pretraining with co-attention; opened the pretrain-then-transfer era for V+L.
  2. 2020 An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale backbone record only ViT: pure Transformer on image patches beats CNNs at scale; the visual backbone of nearly every later VLM.
  3. 2021 Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision contrastive record only CLIP: contrastive image-text pretraining on 400M pairs gives zero-shot classification; the field-defining V+L encoder.
  4. 2021 Scaling Up Visual and Vision-Language Representation Learning With Noisy Text Supervision contrastive record only ALIGN scaled contrastive pretraining to 1.8B noisy alt-text pairs, showing raw web data suffices at scale.

foundational2022

  1. 2022 BLIP: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training for Unified Vision-Language Understanding and Generation pretraining record only Unified understanding+generation pretraining with caption bootstrapping (CapFilt) over noisy web data.
  2. 2022 CoCa: Contrastive Captioners are Image-Text Foundation Models pretraining record only Combined contrastive and captioning losses in one foundation model; long-standing SOTA on retrieval and captioning.
  3. 2022 Flamingo: a Visual Language Model for Few-Shot Learning fusion record only Bridged a frozen LM and vision encoder with gated cross-attention; few-shot multimodal in-context learning.
  4. 2022 PaLI: A Jointly-Scaled Multilingual Language-Image Model pretraining record only Jointly scaled vision and language components (ViT-e + mT5) with multilingual data; evidence for balanced V+L scaling.

expansion2023–2024

  1. 2023 BLIP-2: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training with Frozen Image Encoders and Large Language Models fusion record only Q-Former bridges frozen vision encoders to frozen LLMs; cheap modular recipe behind a generation of open MLLMs.
  2. 2023 InstructBLIP: Towards General-purpose Vision-Language Models with Instruction Tuning instruction record only Systematized visual instruction tuning over BLIP-2 with instruction-aware query extraction across 26 datasets.
  3. 2023 InternVL: Scaling up Vision Foundation Models and Aligning for Generic Visual-Linguistic Tasks frontier record only Scaled the vision encoder to 6B and progressively aligned it with LLMs; the leading open frontier VLM family.
  4. 2023 Qwen-VL: A Versatile Vision-Language Model for Understanding, Localization, Text Reading, and Beyond frontier record only Open frontier VLM line adding grounding and OCR-style reading; the base of the dominant Qwen-VL series.
  5. 2023 Sigmoid Loss for Language Image Pre-Training contrastive record only SigLIP replaced softmax contrastive loss with a pairwise sigmoid, better at small batches; the default open CLIP successor.
  6. 2023 Visual Instruction Tuning instruction record only LLaVA: GPT-4-generated visual instruction data turns CLIP+LLM into a general assistant; the open MLLM recipe.
  7. 2024 Molmo and PixMo: Open Weights and Open Data for State-of-the-Art Vision-Language Models frontier record only Fully open weights AND data (no distillation from proprietary VLMs) at frontier quality; includes pointing supervision.

recent2025

  1. 2025 InternVL3: Exploring Advanced Training and Test-Time Recipes for Open-Source Multimodal Models frontier record only Native multimodal pretraining (joint text+multimodal from scratch) at open frontier quality.
  2. 2025 Qwen2.5-VL Technical Report frontier record only Dynamic-resolution, long-video, document-parsing open VLM; the open-weight frontier reference of 2025.

Reinforcement Learning

Deep reinforcement learning: value-based and policy-gradient methods, self-play and game milestones, world models, exploration, offline RL, sequence-model RL, and preference/verifiable-reward RL feeding modern LLM training.

0 read · 0 of 37 summarized

emergence2013–2015

  1. 2013 Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning value based record only DQN: first agent to learn many Atari games at human level via CNN Q-learning from raw pixels (Nature 2015 follow-up).
  2. 2014 Deterministic Policy Gradient Algorithms policy gradient record only Theoretical groundwork for deterministic actor-critic in continuous action spaces; direct precursor to DDPG.
  3. 2015 Continuous control with deep reinforcement learning policy gradient record only DDPG extended DPG with replay and target networks; breakthrough for high-dimensional continuous control.
  4. 2015 Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning value based record only Decoupled action selection from evaluation to fix DQN overestimation bias; made deep Q-learning reliable.
  5. 2015 Dueling Network Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning value based record only Split the Q-network into state-value and advantage streams, improving performance across Atari.
  6. 2015 High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation policy gradient record only GAE: variance-reduced advantage estimates trading bias against variance; stabilized TRPO and later PPO training.
  7. 2015 Prioritized Experience Replay value based record only Replays high-TD-error transitions more often, accelerating error correction and improving sample efficiency.
  8. 2015 Trust Region Policy Optimization policy gradient record only Stabilized on-policy policy gradients with a KL trust-region constraint, preventing catastrophic policy drops.

foundational2016–2018

  1. 2016 Asynchronous Methods for Deep Reinforcement Learning policy gradient record only A3C used parallel asynchronous actors to decorrelate data without a replay buffer, dramatically boosting learning speed.
  2. 2016 Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search games record only AlphaGo: historic first AI to defeat a world Go champion, combining deep policy/value networks with MCTS.
  3. 2017 A Distributional Perspective on Reinforcement Learning value based record only C51 learned full return distributions instead of expected values; influenced Rainbow and many successors.
  4. 2017 Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences rlhf record only Introduced RLHF - a reward model learned from human preference comparisons replaces hand-crafted rewards.
  5. 2017 Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play with a General Reinforcement Learning Algorithm games record only AlphaZero generalized self-play plus search to master chess, shogi, and Go with one algorithm (Science 2018).
  6. 2017 Mastering the game of Go without human knowledge games record only AlphaGo Zero learned Go from scratch through pure self-play RL plus MCTS, surpassing AlphaGo without human games.
  7. 2017 Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms policy gradient record only Clipped surrogate objective made trust-region-style updates simple and reliable; the most popular deep-RL algorithm.
  8. 2017 Rainbow: Combining Improvements in Deep Reinforcement Learning value based record only Combined six DQN enhancements into one SOTA Atari agent, showing the extensions are largely complementary.
  9. 2018 Addressing Function Approximation Error in Actor-Critic Methods policy gradient record only TD3 fixed DDPG overestimation via twin Q-networks, delayed policy updates, and target smoothing.
  10. 2018 IMPALA: Scalable Distributed Deep-RL with Importance Weighted Actor-Learner Architectures policy gradient record only V-trace off-policy correction let a central learner scale to many distributed actors; shaped large-scale RL systems.
  11. 2018 Learning Latent Dynamics for Planning from Pixels model based record only PlaNet learned latent-space dynamics from images to plan without a known model, enabling strong control from pixels.
  12. 2018 Soft Actor-Critic: Off-Policy Maximum Entropy Deep Reinforcement Learning with a Stochastic Actor policy gradient record only Maximum-entropy off-policy actor-critic with twin critics; SOTA stability and sample efficiency in continuous control.
  13. 2018 World Models model based record only Learned a compact latent model of the environment and trained the policy inside the dream; inspired PlaNet and Dreamer.

expansion2019–2022

  1. 2019 Dota 2 with Large Scale Deep Reinforcement Learning games record only OpenAI Five used massively scaled PPO self-play to defeat the Dota 2 world champions in 5v5.
  2. 2019 Dream to Control: Learning Behaviors by Latent Imagination model based record only Dreamer trained an actor-critic inside a learned latent world model by imagination; big sample-efficiency gains from pixels.
  3. 2019 Grandmaster level in StarCraft II using multi-agent reinforcement learning games record only AlphaStar reached Grandmaster level in StarCraft II via a multi-agent self-play league, defeating professionals.
  4. 2019 Mastering Atari, Go, Chess and Shogi by Planning with a Learned Model model based record only MuZero planned with a learned dynamics model without given rules; superhuman in board games plus SOTA Atari.
  5. 2020 Agent57: Outperforming the Atari Human Benchmark exploration record only First deep-RL agent above the human baseline on all 57 Atari games, pairing NGU exploration with a meta-controller.
  6. 2020 Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning offline rl record only CQL regularizes Q-values to stay conservative on out-of-distribution actions; the anchor algorithm of offline RL.
  7. 2020 Mastering Atari with Discrete World Models model based record only DreamerV2: first model-based agent to reach human-level Atari, matching top model-free methods with equal experience.
  8. 2021 Decision Transformer: Reinforcement Learning via Sequence Modeling sequence rl record only Recast RL as return-conditioned sequence modeling with Transformers, matching SOTA offline RL without value functions.
  9. 2021 First return, then explore exploration record only Go-Explore's remember-and-return strategy solved previously unsolved hard-exploration games like Montezuma's Revenge.
  10. 2021 Open-Ended Learning Leads to Generally Capable Agents games record only XLand: open-ended training across 700k+ tasks produced generally capable agents that generalize to unseen games.
  11. 2022 A Generalist Agent sequence rl record only Gato: a single transformer policy performing 600+ tasks across games, vision, language, and robotics.
  12. 2022 Discovering faster matrix multiplication algorithms with reinforcement learning games record only AlphaTensor's AlphaZero-style search found matrix-multiplication algorithms beating a 50-year-old record.
  13. 2022 Human-level play in the game of Diplomacy by combining language models with strategic reasoning games record only CICERO: first AI with human-level Diplomacy play - a language model plus RL planning; top 10% of human players.

recent2023–2024

  1. 2023 Faster sorting algorithms discovered using deep reinforcement learning games record only AlphaDev discovered faster sorting routines; first RL-invented algorithms merged into the LLVM libc++ standard library.
  2. 2023 Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models model based record only DreamerV3 mastered 150+ diverse tasks with one configuration; first agent to collect Minecraft diamonds from scratch.
  3. 2024 DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models rlhf record only Introduced GRPO, the critic-free PPO variant that became the de facto algorithm for RL with verifiable rewards on LLMs.
